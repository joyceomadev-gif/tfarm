# Commodity Trading Module User Guide

This guide is based on the implemented code in [commodity_trading](commodity_trading) and the functional description in [commodity.md](commodity.md). It focuses on what users can actually do in the module today, how each option works, and which other Odoo modules it depends on.

## 1. What This Module Does

The module adds a commodity trading workflow to Odoo for:

- managing commodity contracts
- creating and pricing fixations
- approving purchase requests before confirmation
- capturing weighbridge and quality control data on receipts
- creating commodity-specific vendor bills and customer invoices
- printing a consolidated commercial invoice report
- tracking exchange rates and currency snapshots on sales orders

## 2. Required Odoo Modules

This module depends on core Odoo apps:

- base for partners, currencies, access control, and sequences
- sale_management for sales orders and customer invoicing
- purchase for purchase orders and vendor bills
- stock for receipts, deliveries, and picking validation
- account for invoice and bill generation

## 3. Installed Menu and Main Records

The module creates the root menu [Commodity Trading](commodity_trading) with a [Contracts](commodity_trading) entry. From there, users work mainly with:

- Commodity Contracts
- Price Fixations
- Purchase Orders
- Sales Orders
- Incoming Stock Pickings

## 4. Commodity Contract Screen

Open a contract from [Contracts](commodity_trading) to manage the commercial agreement.

### Fields you fill in

- Contract Reference: unique contract number generated by sequence
- Customer/Vendor: the partner linked to the contract
- Product: the commodity product being traded
- Contract Type: customer contract or purchase contract
- Contract Quantity: quantity in metric tons
- Contract Currency: currency used for market pricing
- Sales Currency: currency used for customer invoicing
- Procurement Currency: currency used for supplier purchases
- Market Price: base market price in the contract currency
- Differential: premium or discount applied to the market price
- Default Provisional %: default percentage used for provisional fixations

### What the system calculates

- Est. Contract Price = Market Price + Differential
- Total Delivered (Payable MT): total net delivered weight from completed stock receipts
- Total Fixed Qty (MT): sum of confirmed and invoiced fixations
- Unpriced Delivered Qty (MT): delivered quantity minus fixed quantity

### Available action

- Sales Orders: opens all sales orders linked to this contract

### Relation with other modules

- Uses sale_management to show related sales orders
- Uses purchase and stock because contract data is copied into purchase orders and pickings
- Uses account indirectly through invoicing and fixation billing

## 5. Price Fixation Screen

Open a fixation from the contract tab or create it directly to record the commercial pricing step.

### Fixation types

- Provisional: applies a percentage of the market price and acts as a stop-loss style provisional price
- Part Fixation: prices only part of the delivered quantity
- Final Price Fixation: locks the final price for the selected quantity

### Main fields

- Contract: the linked commodity contract
- Fixation Date: pricing date
- Fixed Quantity (MT): quantity being fixed
- Provisional %: used only for provisional fixations
- Current Market Price: snapshot of the market price at fixation time
- Contract Differential: copied from the contract by default
- Calculated Fixation Price: computed fixation value in contract currency
- Fixation Price (Sales Currency): computed value in sales currency
- FX Rate and FX Date: currency conversion snapshot
- Linked Provisional Fixation: used when a final fixation replaces a provisional one

### Available action

- Convert to Final: opens a wizard that turns a provisional fixation into a final fixation

### How conversion works

The conversion wizard asks for the quantity to convert. It creates a new final fixation linked to the original provisional fixation and marks the provisional fixation as converted when the full quantity is moved.

### Relation with other modules

- Uses account for invoice creation from fixations
- Uses sale_management for customer side true-up invoicing
- Uses stock and purchase indirectly when the fixation is tied to received or delivered goods

## 6. Purchase Order Workflow

On purchase orders, the module adds a commodity contract link and a strict approval step.

### How to use it

1. Select the Commodity Contract on the purchase order.
2. The vendor and commodity line are prefilled from the contract.
3. If your user belongs to the Commodity PO Approver group, click Approve Request.
4. After approval, confirm the order.
5. Once receipts are completed, click Create Commodity Bill.

### Available actions

- Approve Request: moves the purchase order to the approved state and clears scheduled approval activities
- Create Commodity Bill: creates a vendor bill from completed receipts that have not yet been billed

### Billing behavior

The bill is built from linked done receipts:

- payable weight is billed at the purchase price
- quality discount weight is billed as a separate zero-price penalty line

### Relation with other modules

- Uses purchase for approval and bill generation
- Uses stock because the bill is created from completed receipts
- Uses account for the resulting vendor bill

## 7. Sales Order Workflow

On sales orders, the module links a commodity contract and adds currency tracking and commodity invoicing tools.

### How to use it

1. Select the Commodity Contract on the sales order.
2. The customer and order lines are prefilled from the contract.
3. Review the exchange rate panel if the order currency differs from the company currency.
4. Use Create Commodity Invoice to invoice confirmed fixations.
5. Use Print Consolidated PDF when all fixations are finalized and invoiced.

### Available actions

- Create Commodity Invoice: creates a customer invoice from confirmed fixations
- Print Consolidated PDF: prints the consolidated commercial invoice report
- + Add Exchange Rate: opens a currency rate form
- Refresh Rates: reloads the latest rates into the order snapshot lines

### Invoicing behavior

The invoice engine supports two paths:

- standard fixation invoicing when the fixation is not linked to a provisional one
- true-up invoicing when a final fixation is linked to a provisional fixation

In the true-up case, the invoice may contain:

- a line for the final quantity at the final price
- a separate line for the unpaid difference on the provisional quantity

### Relation with other modules

- Uses sale_management for sales orders and invoices
- Uses account for customer invoices
- Uses base currency conversion utilities through res.currency
- Uses the custom commodity_trading.currency_rate_line model for snapshot display

## 8. Stock Picking Workflow

The stock picking form is where logistics, weighbridge, and quality control data are entered.

### Logistics tab

Use this tab to record:

- vehicle and truck details
- driver information
- route and destination
- estimated arrival date and notes

### Weighbridge tab

Use this tab for incoming receipts to record:

- Weighbridge IN
- Weighbridge OUT
- bag count
- moisture discount

The system computes:

- gross weight
- bag discount
- net received weight
- payable weight

### Quality Control tab

Use this tab on incoming receipts to assess quality in two steps.

#### Step 1: Evaluate QC Results

This button checks all laboratory rules:

- ADM group: Sieve, FM, Flat Beans, and total ADM sum
- CRM group: BB, Fragment, Cluster, and total CRM sum
- TD group: Mould and Insect, and total TD sum
- Bean Bloc: Slaty, Moisture, and Bean Count

If the lot passes:

- status becomes Passed
- quality discount weight resets to zero
- the record becomes locked

If the lot fails:

- status becomes Failed
- the manager can enter a quality discount weight

#### Step 2: Finalize QC Penalty

Use this button after entering the discount weight on a failed lot. It locks the receipt so it can no longer be edited.

### Relation with other modules

- Uses stock because it extends incoming receipts
- Uses purchase for purchase receipt billing
- Uses account for vendor bill creation from receipts

## 9. Currency Rate Lines on Sales Orders

Each sales order can show a live snapshot of currency rates.

### Available actions

- Add Exchange Rate: opens a new res.currency.rate form prefilled with the order currency
- Refresh Rates: rebuilds the currency rate snapshot lines for all active currencies

### Why this matters

This is useful when the contract currency, sales currency, and company currency are different. It helps users see the rates used for the order without leaving the sales form.

## 10. Consolidated Invoice Report

The Print Consolidated PDF action generates a QWeb commercial invoice from the sales order.

### What the report shows

- company identity and logo
- customer details and invoice number
- contract number and commodity
- contract tonnage and delivered quantity
- all fixation records on the contract
- posted customer invoice lines
- total quantity, average price, total value, and 95% value receivable summary

### When it is allowed

The report only prints when:

- the sales order is linked to a commodity contract
- the contract has fixations
- all fixations are already finalized and invoiced

## 11. User Roles and Access

The module defines a Commodity PO Approver group.

### Who needs this role

Users who must approve purchase requests before confirmation.

### What the role can do

- see and use the Approve Request button on purchase orders
- move purchase orders into the approved state

## 13. Important Implementation Notes

The documentation in [commodity.md](commodity.md) is broader than the current implementation. The code does not include a separate laboratory wizard, a dedicated dashboard model, or a full stop-loss/provisional pricing engine beyond the implemented fixation conversion and pricing calculations. This user guide therefore follows the actual models, methods, and buttons that exist in the repository.

## 14. Suggested User Flow

1. Create a commodity contract.
2. Add one or more price fixations.
3. Use purchase orders for supplier intake and approval.
4. Receive stock and evaluate QC on incoming pickings.
5. Create the vendor bill from completed receipts.
6. Use sales orders to create customer invoices from fixations.
7. Print the consolidated invoice once all fixations are invoiced.
